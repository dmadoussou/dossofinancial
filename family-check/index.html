    UNDERSTAND THE DIFFERENT JOBS

    One concern. Different kinds of support.

    Explore what each resource is designed to do—and which details you should verify.

    Emergency savings

    Available savings may bridge expenses without a claim qualification. How long they last depends on your gap and competing needs. This tool assumes no growth, inflation, or unexpected costs.

    Disability income coverage

    May replace some income following a covered disability. Definitions, waiting periods, amounts, benefit duration, and tax treatment vary. It does not automatically cover job loss.

    Term life insurance

    Provides death benefit protection for a specified period under the contract’s terms. Generally has no cash value. Some policies offer accelerated benefit riders; check the actual policy.

    Living benefit riders

    Some riders permit access to part of a death benefit after a qualifying event. They generally reduce remaining benefits. Eligibility and payout terms vary; they are not automatically disability or long-term care insurance.

    Permanent insurance and IUL

    Eligible permanent policies, including whole life and indexed universal life, may accumulate cash value. IUL crediting may reference an index; it is not direct index investing. Funding, charges, crediting limits, and policy maintenance matter.

    Cash-value access

    Withdrawals and loans have terms and costs. Loans carry interest and can affect values, death benefits, lapse risk, and taxes. Cash value takes time to build and is not assumed to be emergency savings in this check.
