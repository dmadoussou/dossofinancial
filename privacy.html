Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use dossofinancial.com or contact me through the website.

Effective September 21, 2026

dossofinancial.com is operated by Madoussou Dosso, an independent life insurance producer. I respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to you and operate this website.

Information you may provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and a message. If you choose to schedule a conversation through Calendly, the information you provide there is handled through Calendly's service and privacy practices.

How information is used

  • To respond to your inquiry or request.
  • To coordinate or follow up on a conversation you requested.
  • To operate, maintain, and protect the website and related communications.
  • To keep records when reasonably necessary for business, compliance, or legal purposes.

I do not sell personal information submitted through this website.

Service providers

The website may rely on third party service providers to host the site, deliver form submissions, and provide scheduling functionality. Information submitted through the contact form is processed by the form delivery service used by this site so it can be sent to me by email. If you use the scheduling link, Calendly processes the information you submit to that service under its own terms and privacy policy.

Website and technical information

Hosting and internet service providers may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, and security data as part of delivering and protecting the website. This site does not currently use advertising pixels or behavioral analytics that I control.

How long information is kept

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business records, or to meet applicable legal or compliance requirements.

Your choices

You may contact me to ask about information you submitted through this website, request a correction, or request deletion where deletion is not restricted by a legal or compliance obligation.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no website, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

External links

This website may link to third party services or websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to mdosso@dossofinancial.com or 240.702.1772.
